# Camera sources

74 selected sources in 12 cities. The viewer rotates these automatically; this document is the attribution/source reference, not the screensaver interface.

Media URLs were checked on 2026-10-11. This confirms reachability at that time, not continuous availability or video playback for every source. Broken media is skipped with a ten-minute backoff. Images refresh every 15 seconds while selected; source capture intervals vary. TfL videos are short recent clips, labeled as loops. Direct HLS and public webcam embeds are labeled live video only after playback starts.
